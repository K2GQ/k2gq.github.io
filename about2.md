## Welcome to the Roseland Amateur Radio Club

<figure class="rarc-feature-image">
  <img src="/assets/img/log-1978.jpg" alt="Historical Roseland Amateur Radio Club logbook" loading="lazy">
</figure>

The Roseland Amateur Radio Club is based in Roseland, Essex County, New Jersey. We bring together people who enjoy amateur radio, communications, electronics, and learning from one another. Our members include Technician, General, and Amateur Extra license holders, and we welcome newcomers who are just getting started.

As an [ARRL-affiliated club](https://www.arrl.org/), we help aspiring operators prepare for their license exams and encourage members to explore all that amateur radio has to offer.

## Our History

<figure class="rarc-portrait">
  <img src="/assets/img/doc-k2gq.jpg" alt="Dr. Milton I. 'Doc' Schwalbe, founder of the club" loading="lazy">
  <figcaption>Dr. Milton I. “Doc” Schwalbe, former holder of K2GQ</figcaption>
</figure>

The club traces its beginnings to **1946**, when it was founded in Irvington, New Jersey, by Dr. Milton I. “Doc” Schwalbe. Originally known as the **Irvington Radio Amateur Club (IRAC)**, it was later called the Irvington-Roseland Amateur Radio Club before taking its present name. The club's call sign, **K2GQ**, was formerly held by Doc Schwalbe, as noted in [*QST*, April 1961](https://www.worldradiohistory.com/Archive-DX/QST/60s/QST-1961-04.pdf).

Reports in *QST*, the ARRL's magazine, offer a glimpse into those early years. In 1955, the club held regular meetings, offered weekly Morse code and radio theory classes, and operated a Saturday amateur radio net on **28.7 MHz**. One report noted that the classes drew **20 to 25 people per session**.

Members were also active in **ARRL Field Day**. A photograph published in the December 1958 issue of *QST* shows Irvington club members gathered around a generator, working to keep their Field Day operation running. The club affiliated with the ARRL in 1958, and its longstanding traditions of learning, fellowship, and public service continue today.

## The Club Today

<img class="rarc-side-image" src="/assets/img/gonset.jpg" alt="Vintage Gonset amateur radio equipment" loading="lazy">

In 2015, the club renewed its focus on emergency communications and community service, as described in this [local news article](https://essexnewsdaily.com/news/roseland/explore-the-world-through-a-ham-radio/). Members continue to share an interest in radio, technology, and helping their neighbors.

<div class="rarc-clear"></div>

<img class="rarc-side-image" src="/assets/img/ftdx10_club.jpg" alt="Amateur radio equipment at a club gathering" loading="lazy">

Each year, our volunteers provide [communications support for community events](/2024-09-21-roseland-run/), including charity runs in Essex County. We also help new operators build their knowledge and confidence through mentoring and shared experience.

We invite you to join us at an [upcoming meeting](/calendar/) to meet members and learn more about amateur radio. Visitors and new members are always welcome.
